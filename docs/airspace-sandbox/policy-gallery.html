同一套过境规则，四个街区会怎样？

What happens when the same through-block rule meets four neighbourhoods?

这不是配送动画，也不是在演示某座城市已经获批可以飞。 我们问的是一件更窄、也更容易看懂的事：一架无人机如果必须保持同一高度，从 500 米见方街区的一侧进去、对侧出去，六套“离楼 / 离人多远”的规则会不会把它堵住。

This is not a delivery animation, and it is not a claim that any city has approved this operation. The question is narrower: if a drone must hold a constant height, enter a 500-metre square on one side and leave on the opposite side, do six keep-away rules still leave a path?

Pick a neighbourhood, then a rule. Coloured ribbons are paths. Pink is the keep-away band at the height you are looking at. The 4×6 cards are every case we computed. Green numbers are packed trips in one modelled hour, not an official limit.

我们在看什么飞行？

只看过境平飞。飞机从街区北边进、南边出，或从东边进、西边出。飞行过程中高度不变：30 米就是 30 米飞完，不会先爬升再下降去躲楼。 我们没有模拟从楼下起飞、送到窗口、再降落。那是另一类问题，需要另外的高度变化规则。

This page only shows level through-traffic: in from the north and out to the south, or in from the east and out to the west. Altitude does not change during the crossing. We do not model take-off from the street, a drop at a window, or landing. That would be a different height profile.

每个街区是 500 米见方的研究区，外面再围一圈邻楼，免得路刚走到边界就被切掉。黑线方框是研究区。浅色建筑在框外，深色建筑在框内。

Each neighbourhood is a 500 m research square, plus neighbouring buildings around it so a path is not cut at the edge. The black square is the study area. Pale buildings sit outside it; darker buildings sit inside.

四个街区，故意选得很不一样

它们不是“四个航空业最发达的城市”，而是四种盖法：高而挤、中等但密、高但疏、中等且疏。 我们要看的是形态本身，不是某座城市的无人机产业是否成熟。

These are four ways of building a city, not a ranking of drone markets: tall-and-tight, mid-and-dense, tall-and-open, mid-and-open.

六套规则：数字看起来像法规，但这里是研究情景

每一套都来自真实条款的某一小段（离楼 10 米、离楼 30 米、离人 30 米、离建筑 50 米），再接到我们能算的几何上。 页面上的结果不是合规证书：高度限制、地理围栏、视距、机与机的间隔、审批类别都没有完整做进去。 实验室 5 米尤其不是中国或香港的法律。

Each package borrows one measurable piece of a real rule and maps it onto geometry we can compute. The pictures are not certificates of compliance. Ceilings, geofences, visual-line-of-sight, aircraft-to-aircraft spacing and approval class are not fully represented. The laboratory 5 m case is not Chinese or Hong Kong law.

每张卡片是“一个街区 × 一套规则”。绿色是还能对穿，并写出这一小时装进了多少架次。 橙色是：要么只剩下转弯、没有对穿，要么门口看似开着但连不成路。红色是对边门口已经同时打不开。 点卡片即可把右边的三维切过去。

Each card is one neighbourhood times one rule. Green means through-block paths exist, with the packed trips in that modelled hour. Amber means only corner-turning remains, or a crack that does not become a path. Red means opposite doorways are not open together. Click a card to drive the 3D view.

可以对穿 转弯或路不通 对边过不去
请不要把香港 338、东京 2,070 当成官方容量 同一套实验室 5 米规则下，洛杉矶大约 22,320 架次/小时，上海大约 23,760，东京大约 2,070，香港大约 338。 洛杉矶和上海与我们更早的对照更接近；香港和东京这一格明显偏低，而且香港在更严的“水平 10 米、只飞 30 米”情景里反而排出了约 1,035 架次。 这不表示香港法例让空域变宽。它表示：这次用的规划器、重建后的地块几何，和旧的公开基线不是同一套装置。页面照实显示数字，但香港 338 与东京 2,070 需要单独复查，不能拿去讲政策故事。

Under the same laboratory 5 m rule, Los Angeles is about 22,320 trips/hour and Shanghai about 23,760, while Tokyo is about 2,070 and Hong Kong about 338. LA and Shanghai match earlier checks more closely. Hong Kong and Tokyo look too low, and Hong Kong even packs more trips under the stricter 10 m / 30 m case (~1,035). That is not “Hong Kong law creates more airspace”. It means this planner and rebuilt geometry are not the old published baseline. The numbers are shown honestly; 338 and 2,070 need a separate check before any policy sentence.

三维图里的颜色在说什么？

彩色带子是候选航线，颜色表示巡航高度：30 米偏青绿，50 米偏蓝，70 米偏紫，90 米偏粉，110 米偏黄。 默认只画对穿：北↔南或东↔西。转弯（例如东进北出）可以打开，但那不是“穿过街区”。 小球沿着被这一小时真正用到的航线走动，只是为了让路可被看见，不是 2 万架飞机同时在飞。

Ribbon colour is cruise height: teal 30 m, blue 50 m, violet 70 m, pink 90 m, yellow 110 m. Through-block paths are on by default. Corner paths can be switched on; they are not a crossing of the block. The moving dots follow routes used in that hour so the path is visible. They are not 20,000 drones at once.

粉红色薄层画在你选的高度上，表示“离建筑（或离人行带）太近”。 香港风格的水平规则会把楼顶正上方也涂上粉红——因为地图上的侧向距离仍不够。 日本风格的直线距离会在你飞得够高时缩小这圈粉红，因为竖直方向已经贡献了距离。 “离人 30 米”的粉红在每个高度都在，人行带正上方也不能抄过去。

The pink sheet sits at the height you selected and marks air that is too close to buildings, or to the people stand-in. A Hong Kong-style horizontal rule paints the roof as well, because the map gap is still too small. A Japan-style straight-line rule shrinks that pink when you are high enough, because vertical separation already counts. The 30 m people rule stays in force at every height: you cannot overfly the sidewalk stand-in.

数字怎么读

架次/小时是排班器在一小时里、在机与机还要互相让开的条件下，装进去的行程数。模型里两架飞机的侧向间隔大约 20 米、高度间隔大约 10 米。这个数字包含对穿和转弯。

Trips per hour is how many journeys the scheduler packed into one hour while aircraft still keep about 20 m sideways and 10 m vertically from each other. The count includes through-block and corner-turning trips.

完成架次是这一小时录像里真正飞完的趟数，通常和架次/小时非常接近。

Completed trips are the journeys that actually finished in that one-hour tape. They are usually close to the hourly rate.

分贝（A 计权）是：假如这些飞机真按这一小时飞，建筑表面按面积平均的声音大小。只在对穿或排班成功的格子上才有。没有现场录音，也没有把整座城市的人口加权进去。

dB(A) is the area-weighted sound level on building surfaces if those trips really flew for that hour. It exists only for cells that produced a schedule. It is not a field recording, and it is not weighted by city population.

“过不去”是什么意思？

它的意思是：在这套规则和这些平飞高度上，不能从街区一侧进、对侧出。 不是飞机撞了楼，不是天上没有空气，也不是这座城市永远不能做无人机。 换一种获批运行、换一条走廊、或者允许爬升，结论都可以变。我们这一页故意不换那些条件，好让四个街区被同一把尺量。

It means: under this rule and these level cruise heights, you cannot enter one side of the block and leave the opposite side. It is not a crash, it is not “no sky”, and it is not a lifetime ban on drones in that city. A different approval class, a dedicated corridor, or a climbing path could change the outcome. This page holds those things fixed on purpose, so the four forms are measured with one ruler.

有时对边门口还开着，格子上却连不成线——卡片会写成“路不通”。 有时排班器找到了转弯的活，但对穿仍是零——卡片会写成“仅转弯”。这两种都算过境失败，我们把它们留在表里，不删掉。

Sometimes opposite doorways still look open, yet the grid has no connecting path — the card says the path does not exist. Sometimes the scheduler finds corner-turning work, but through-block traffic is still zero — the card says corner only. Both count as through-block failure. They stay in the table; they are not dropped.

这一页没有做的事

没有从地面起飞和送到阳台。没有真的人行道或人口地图（“离人”只用楼外 8 米环带代替）。 没有把整套香港、日本或英国法条逐条判完。没有声称 22,320 架次/小时是任何司法辖区已经批准的运行。 原来的沙盘仍在，用来看单块地的交互计算；这一页专门把六套规则和四个例子摊开，让没参加过讨论的人也能把故事看完。

No take-off from the ground, no balcony delivery, no real sidewalk or population map (the people rule uses an 8 m belt around buildings), no full legal opinion, and no claim that 22,320 trips/hour is approved anywhere. The original sandbox remains for interactive computation on one block. This page exists so someone who was not in the research conversation can still follow the through-block story.
